# Closed record carriers

The existing `RC-CLOSED-FIELD-TRANSFER-2` certificate already elides owner loans for `rt = it.rt; helper(mut rt, scalar...); it.rt = rt`, but emits a raw parent copy and two field relocations with zeroing and journal guards. `RC-FIELD-INOUT-1` refines that certificate for one direct record-inout call. The original source stays owning and rooted; the call receives the address of its inline field through the existing ABI, and the ghost target never acquires an owner. Normal leaf COW, callee record rebinding, allocation and traps remain unchanged. No new guest syntax, unchecked access or frontend instruction rewrite is involved.

The stricter proof requires a sole target definition/reference and only its known store-back read. The interval is straight, has exactly one direct guest call, plain other arguments and a plain result, and permits only scalar/local argument preparation. It refuses parent reads/rebinding, branches, escapes, multiple references/calls, nonplain argument preparation, callbacks, transitive indirect/host calls, counted execution and the original certificate's alias/live-view/receiver-scope exclusions. Callee branches and early returns retain their normal lowering. A bounded graph walk admits at most 256 functions and 65,536 instructions. Tracing GC and original bytecode retain the extraction and store-back.

The small carrier probe emits three blits before and zero after (129 to 90 IL lines). The joined Lox source admits 17 sites across 14 `interpreter::` functions. These are emission facts, not a measured performance claim. The parallel flat interpreter source can remove these carriers independently; central measurements compare the compiler variants separately.
