**Command and result:**
- `./bin/mylang check project/main.my` → exit 0, no diagnostics.
- `./bin/mylang run project/main.my -- 41` → `38`; `-- abc` → `-1`.

Also verified `0`→`-3`, `-5`→`-8`, `3.5`→`-1`, `""`→`-1`, matching `public/expected.json`.
